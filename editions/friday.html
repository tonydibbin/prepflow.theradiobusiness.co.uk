04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    The delightful art of 'puddle jumping' is being rediscovered as a simple joy by adults.
    Forget umbrellas, people are finding that a good pair of wellies and a proper splash can be surprisingly uplifting. It's a throwback to childhood fun, proving that some simple pleasures never lose their charm.
  • 2
    New research suggests that 'colouring in' can significantly reduce stress levels in busy grown-ups.
    It's not just for kids anymore! Taking out a colouring book and some pencils is proving to be a wonderfully mindful way to unwind after a long day, letting your creative side take over.
  • 3
    The satisfying ritual of 'dusting off old board games' for family nights is seeing a resurgence.
    Instead of screen time, many families are turning back to classic board games for some good old-fashioned fun. It's a brilliant way to connect, laugh, and maybe get a little competitive!
  • 4
    There's a growing trend for 'micro-adventures' – short, accessible trips close to home.
    People are realising that you don't need to go far to have an adventure; a hike in a local park or a night camping in the garden can provide a real sense of escape and discovery.
  • 5
    The simple pleasure of 'watching the clouds drift by' is being advocated as a mindful moment.
    Taking a few minutes to gaze at the ever-changing shapes in the sky can be incredibly calming. It's a free and easy way to practise mindfulness and appreciate the natural world around us.
  • 6
    Home bakers are embracing 'the humble scone' as the ultimate comfort food to perfect.
    From plain to fruit, with jam and cream, the quest for the perfect scone is capturing the hearts (and ovens!) of many. It's a timeless treat that brings a little bit of British tradition into any home.
  • 7
    The charming habit of 'leaving out food for garden birds' is bringing joy to countless households.
    Filling up bird feeders and watching the feathered visitors is a wonderful way to connect with nature from your own window. It's a small act of kindness that brings a lot of pleasure.
