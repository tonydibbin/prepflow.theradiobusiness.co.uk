04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    Pubs report a Tuesday boom — the new 'mid-week meet' is the fastest-growing dining occasion.
    Operators say WFH-ers like getting out the house mid-week without the weekend crowds. Tuesday wine sales are up almost 30%.
  • 2
    Almost 70% of UK households now have a smart speaker — but the most common use is still 'asking what time it is'.
    Researchers say we under-use the devices we own. Setting timers, playing music and weather queries make up two thirds of all commands.
  • 3
    The 'silver gamer' is the fastest-growing demographic in UK gaming — over-65s spent £180 million on games last year.
    Cosy life-sim titles like Stardew Valley and Animal Crossing dominate. Console makers are reportedly designing larger UI for the market.
  • 4
    More of us are taking up 'fake commutes' — a short walk before and after working from home.
    Psychologists say the made-up journey helps separate work from home life. Some people even pop to a shop and back to 'arrive'.
  • 5
    Britain's appetite for 'cosy' nights in is reshaping Friday nights.
    Takeaway and streaming bookings now peak earlier in the evening, as more of us swap the big night out for a big night in.
  • 6
    Local 'repair cafés', where volunteers fix broken items for free, are spreading across the UK.
    Toasters, lamps and zips are the most common patients. Organisers say the biggest barrier is convincing people their old kettle is worth saving.
  • 7
    Charity shops are reporting a surge in both donations and shoppers as 'pre-loved' goes mainstream.
    Younger shoppers in particular are hunting for one-off finds. Volunteers say the most donated item by a mile is the unworn 'someday' outfit.
