    The simple act of caring for something green, combined with the gentle flow of water, provides a tranquil break from screens and busy schedules.

    A charming movement sees people 'collecting unusual old postcards' from forgotten holiday spots and historic towns.
    It's a lovely way to connect with the past and discover snippets of history and travel from yesteryear, often with fascinating handwritten messages on the back.

    The satisfying ritual of 'sharpening pencils by hand' is making a quiet comeback among those seeking a moment of analogue calm.
