04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    British gardeners are finding new joy in cultivating 'edible flowers', adding colour and flavour to their cooking.
    From nasturtiums in salads to lavender in desserts, these blooms are turning everyday meals into a visual and culinary delight. It's a lovely way to bring a bit of the garden to the plate.
  • 2
    The trend for 'slow crafting' is gaining popularity, encouraging us to take our time with creative projects.
    Think knitting, pottery, or even intricate papercrafts – it's all about enjoying the process rather than rushing to finish. A wonderful antidote to our fast-paced lives, don't you think?
  • 3
    Many Brits are discovering the simple pleasure of 'porch light reading', enjoying a good book under the gentle glow of an evening light.
    It's a quiet moment of calm, often accompanied by a cuppa, as the day winds down. A lovely way to unwind and enjoy the milder evenings.
  • 4
    The humble 'picnic blanket' is getting a stylish makeover, becoming a must-have accessory for outdoor gatherings.
    From vintage-inspired patterns to waterproof designs, these blankets are making al fresco dining even more comfortable and chic. Perfect for a park visit or a garden tea party.
  • 5
    A new appreciation for 'local history walks' is emerging, with communities exploring the hidden stories of their own neighbourhoods.
    Guided tours or self-led strolls are uncovering fascinating facts about buildings, streets, and notable residents. It's a wonderful way to connect with your local area.
  • 6
    The delightful practice of 'seed swapping' is blooming, allowing gardeners to share rare and interesting plant varieties.
    Local events and online communities are connecting enthusiasts, promoting biodiversity and the joy of growing something unique. A lovely green exchange!
  • 7
    More people are embracing 'mindful photography', focusing on capturing beauty in everyday moments.
    It's not about perfect shots, but about really seeing and appreciating the small details around us, from a dewdrop on a leaf to the play of light. A lovely way to practice presence.
