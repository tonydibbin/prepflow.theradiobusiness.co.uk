    There's nothing quite like the smell of freshly baked bread wafting through the house, is there? More and more people are enjoying the therapeutic process and delicious results.

    It's a wonderful way to expand your indoor jungle, share green-fingered tips, and connect with your community. Plus, it's a very affordable way to get new plant varieties!
