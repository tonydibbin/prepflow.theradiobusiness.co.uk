04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    A study suggests humming or singing badly in the car genuinely lifts your mood.
    Researchers link it to deep breathing and a small dose of joy. Fellow drivers at the lights may not share the enthusiasm.
  • 2
    Brits spend an average of 38 minutes a day deciding what to watch — and 12 of those are spent scrolling Netflix.
    'Watch-paralysis' has its own subreddit. Streamers are quietly experimenting with 'shuffle' buttons that just play something.
  • 3
    More people are writing 'done lists' instead of to-do lists to feel better about their day.
    Noting what you actually achieved, however small, apparently beats staring at everything you didn't. 'Got dressed' counts.
  • 4
    British baby names are getting shorter again — three- and four-letter names are dominating the registrar.
    Top picks this year: Noa, Ezra, Ada and Bo. Long, multi-syllable Victorian names like Theodora are sliding back down the chart.
  • 5
    Loneliness is now considered a bigger health risk than smoking 15 cigarettes a day, according to NHS England.
    Social prescribing — GPs referring patients to community activities — is rolling out nationwide. Walking groups, book clubs and choirs are reportedly oversubscribed.
  • 6
    More of us are learning the names of the birds in our gardens, apps report.
    Bird-ID apps have made spotting easier. The robin remains the nation's clear favourite, mostly for its cheek.
  • 7
    A survey says most of us have a 'signature dish' we bring out whenever guests come round.
    Chilli, lasagne and a roast top the list. Very few of us, it turns out, have a second dish ready if the first goes wrong.
