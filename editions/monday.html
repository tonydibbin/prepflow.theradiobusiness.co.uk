04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    A poll finds the nation can't agree on the correct way to make a cup of tea.
    Milk first or last remains a genuine source of household tension. The one thing we agree on: someone else should make it.
  • 2
    One in three of us has had a 'sleep divorce' — sleeping in a separate bed from our partner.
    Reasons: snoring (45%), different schedules (30%), restless legs, and one bizarre 12% who say their partner 'talks to the cat in their sleep'.
  • 3
    A poll finds most people have a song that instantly makes them feel like dancing, even in the kitchen.
    Kitchen discos are a quiet national pastime. The wooden spoon remains the microphone of choice.
  • 4
    Jigsaw puzzles are still selling in huge numbers, years after their lockdown boom.
    1,000-piece designs are the sweet spot. The most dreaded puzzle remains anything that's mostly sky.
  • 5
    A study says the smell of fresh bread is one of the few things almost everyone finds comforting.
    Supermarkets have long known it — the in-store bakery is no accident. Few of us escape without an unplanned loaf.
  • 6
    A poll says most people can't remember the last time they wrote a letter by hand.
    Yet greetings-card sales remain strong — we'll happily sign our name, just not write a full page.
  • 7
    The number of people learning to play an instrument as an adult is rising fast.
    Piano and guitar lead the way, with online lessons making it easier. The hardest part, learners say, is finding somewhere to practise without an audience.
