    Beyond the traditional park, people are enjoying packed lunches in their back gardens, on living room floors, or even in local beauty spots they've never visited before. It's all about making everyday moments special.

    Children and adults alike are enjoying spotting and gathering colourful leaves during autumn walks. They're often used for crafts, decorations, or simply admired for their natural beauty.

    Instead of throwing things away, many are learning new skills to repair clothes, furniture, or household goods. It's a creative way to reduce waste and give beloved items a new lease of life.
