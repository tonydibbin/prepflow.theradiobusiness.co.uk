04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    A new trend sees people embracing 'upcycled fashion' by transforming old clothes into stylish new outfits.
    Fashionistas are finding joy and creativity in customising pre-loved garments, giving them a unique flair and extending their lifecycle. It's a wonderful way to be sustainable and express personal style.
  • 2
    The simple art of 'doodling' is being recognised for its surprising cognitive benefits.
    Research suggests that those seemingly idle sketches can actually help improve concentration, memory, and creative problem-solving. So next time you're in a meeting, don't feel guilty about that little drawing!
  • 3
    There's a growing appreciation for the quiet charm of 'canal boat holidays' as a way to unwind.
    Swapping bustling streets for tranquil waterways, families and couples are discovering the joy of slow travel, navigating Britain's scenic canals and experiencing a unique sense of peace.
  • 4
    The comforting ritual of 'making your own bread' is seeing a delightful resurgence in kitchens across the country.
    From rustic sourdoughs to simple loaves, home bakers are finding immense satisfaction in the aroma and taste of freshly baked bread. It’s a truly wholesome activity that brings warmth to any home.
  • 5
    Gardeners are increasingly turning to 'native wildflowers' to boost local biodiversity.
    Planting species that naturally thrive in the UK not only creates beautiful displays but also provides vital habitats and food sources for local pollinators like bees and butterflies, enriching our ecosystems.
  • 6
    The satisfying hobby of 'jigsaw puzzles' is proving to be a popular stress-buster for adults.
    Stepping away from screens and engaging with a tangible challenge, many are rediscovering the calm focus that comes from piecing together an intricate image. It's a lovely way to unwind and feel accomplished.
  • 7
    An increasing number of people are discovering the simple pleasure of 'foraging for wild berries and edibles' in local hedgerows and woodlands.
    With a keen eye and responsible approach, enthusiasts are enjoying the thrill of finding natural treasures like blackberries, elderflowers, and wild garlic, turning them into delicious homemade treats.
