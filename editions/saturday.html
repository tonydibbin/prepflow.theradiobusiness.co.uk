04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    Cash is making a small comeback — ATM withdrawals are up 14% on this time last year.
    Bank analysts credit budget-conscious households using cash to stay disciplined. The 'envelope method' is back trending on TikTok.
  • 2
    Jigsaw puzzles are still selling in huge numbers, years after their lockdown boom.
    1,000-piece designs are the sweet spot. The most dreaded puzzle remains anything that's mostly sky.
  • 3
    Brits are apparently buying more houseplants than ever — and naming them.
    A garden survey found nearly a third of plant owners have given their plants names, and a surprising number apologise to them when they forget to water.
  • 4
    The humble flask is having a comeback, with sales up sharply as people try to cut café spending.
    Retailers say the big winner is the 'keeps drinks hot for 12 hours' bottle — though most of us still drink it within the first one.
  • 5
    Brits now spend an average of 14 hours a week on streaming — more than cooking, cleaning and exercising combined.
    The biggest jump is in the over-55s, who used to be the holdouts for live telly. 'Second-screening' means we pay less attention than ever.
  • 6
    Britain's most-stolen supermarket item in 2026 isn't meat or cheese — it's olive oil, with theft up 220% year on year.
    Prices have nearly doubled in two years. Some shops have fitted security tags to single bottles, others have moved premium brands behind the counter — like cigarettes.
  • 7
    More households are growing herbs on the windowsill to cut grocery bills.
    Basil and mint are the favourites. Survival rates, gardeners admit, are 'mixed' — the basil rarely makes it past a fortnight.
