Quick hits

  • 1
    A delightful trend of 'community book swaps' is flourishing, encouraging neighbours to share their favourite reads.
    Little free libraries are popping up in front gardens and public spaces, creating charming hubs for literary exchange and connection. It's a lovely way to discover new authors without spending a penny.

    Families and friends are packing blankets and snacks to enjoy al fresco dining under the night sky, often with a telescope or binoculars handy. It’s a wonderful blend of good food and cosmic wonder.
  • 3
    More people are embracing the rewarding craft of 'basket weaving', creating beautiful and practical items from natural materials.
    Workshops are seeing a surge in popularity as participants learn ancient techniques to turn reeds and willow into bespoke baskets, proving that traditional skills are still very much in vogue.
  • 4
    The charming hobby of 'train spotting' is experiencing a quiet renaissance, attracting new generations to platforms across the country.
    Enthusiasts, young and old, are taking to railway stations with notebooks and cameras, enjoying the thrill of spotting specific locomotives and capturing their power and beauty. All aboard for a bit of nostalgia!
  • 5
    A new appreciation for the simple artistry of 'pressed flower art' is blooming among craft enthusiasts.
    People are carefully collecting and preserving blossoms to create intricate pictures, cards, and decorative items. It’s a lovely way to capture the beauty of nature year-round.
  • 6
    The soothing practice of 'wild swimming' is gaining popularity, with more Brits taking to rivers, lakes, and the sea for a refreshing dip.
    Beyond the physical benefits, participants often speak of the mental clarity and connection to nature that comes from immersing oneself in natural waters. Just remember to be safe and check conditions!
  • 7
    There's a growing buzz around 'retro computing clubs', where enthusiasts gather to restore and play with vintage computer systems.
    These clubs are a nostalgic trip down memory lane for some and a fascinating historical journey for others, preserving the early days of personal computing. Dig out that old ZX Spectrum!
