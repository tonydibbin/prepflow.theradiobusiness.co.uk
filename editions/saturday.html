04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    The enduring appeal of board games is seeing a delightful resurgence in living rooms across the nation.
    Families and friends are rediscovering the joy of unplugged entertainment, gathering around the kitchen table for classic and new board game challenges. It’s a lovely way to connect and create shared memories, away from screens.
  • 2
    Crafting enthusiasts are embracing the charm of 'upcycled book art', transforming old novels into beautiful decorative pieces.
    From intricate paper sculptures to folded book landscapes, these creative projects give beloved but no longer read books a wonderful new lease of life. It's a sustainable and artistic hobby.
  • 3
    New research suggests that the gentle rhythm of 'knitting or crocheting' can significantly reduce stress and improve focus.
    The repetitive, meditative motions involved in these textile crafts are proving to be a fantastic way to unwind after a busy day. Many are finding it a very mindful activity.
  • 4
    The comforting tradition of 'afternoon tea' is experiencing a wonderful revival, from grand hotels to local cafes.
    People are treating themselves to delicate sandwiches, scones with jam and cream, and a pot of their favourite brew. It's a lovely excuse to slow down and enjoy a bit of indulgence.
  • 5
    Community gardens are flourishing, with more people than ever getting their hands dirty to grow local produce together.
    These shared green spaces are not only providing fresh fruit and vegetables but also fostering strong neighbourhood bonds. It's a truly wholesome activity for all ages.
  • 6
    The simple pleasure of 'reading a physical map' is making a quirky comeback, especially for road trippers and hikers.
    There's a certain satisfaction in unfolding a large map and tracing your route, rather than relying solely on satellite navigation. It adds a touch of adventure to any journey.
  • 7
    A charming trend for 'local history walking tours' is captivating residents and visitors alike.
    These guided strolls reveal the hidden stories and fascinating past of our towns and villages, often unearthing surprising facts about familiar streets. It's a wonderful way to connect with your local heritage.
