    A quirky new trend sees people creating 'miniature indoor gardens' in unconventional containers.

    Even a small friendly gesture helps to build connections and create a more welcoming atmosphere in local areas.

    The surprising joy of 'board game cafes' is growing, offering a relaxed social alternative to traditional pubs.
    These venues provide a fantastic way for friends and families to connect over classic and new board games, enjoying good food and company.

    Scientists have discovered that certain plants emit a 'squeaking sound' when they are thirsty.
