04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    A new study suggests that taking a 'digital detox' can significantly improve sleep quality and reduce stress.
    Researchers found that even a short break from screens before bed helped participants fall asleep faster and feel more refreshed in the morning.
  • 2
    The trend for 'slow tourism' is growing, with more people choosing to immerse themselves in local cultures rather than rushing through destinations.
    This mindful approach to travel encourages longer stays and a deeper connection with the places visited, focusing on experiences over checklists.
  • 3
    Experts are noting a rise in 'intergenerational hobbies', where different age groups come together to share skills and interests.
    From knitting circles to technology workshops, these activities foster unique bonds and a rich exchange of knowledge between generations.
  • 4
    A study reveals that 'singing in a choir' can boost mood and strengthen social connections.
    The act of singing in harmony with others releases endorphins and creates a strong sense of community, proving beneficial for mental well-being.
  • 5
    There's a growing interest in 'heritage crafts', as people seek to learn traditional skills like pottery, weaving, and woodworking.
    This revival not only preserves ancient techniques but also offers a mindful and rewarding escape from modern life.
  • 6
    New research indicates that regularly spending time near 'water features' like rivers or fountains can reduce anxiety.
    The calming sounds and visual appeal of water have a therapeutic effect, helping to lower stress levels and promote relaxation.
  • 7
    The popularity of 'guerilla gardening' is on the rise, with communities transforming neglected public spaces into green oases.
    This grassroots movement brings neighbours together to beautify their local areas, often using hardy plants to create vibrant displays.
