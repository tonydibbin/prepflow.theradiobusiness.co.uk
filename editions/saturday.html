01 — News

Today's top stories

  • 1
    The Prime Minister has ruled out a snap general election, saying it would only throw the country into a 'backward-looking process'.
    Andy Burnham told the BBC he isn't tempted to call an early poll. He believes the focus should be on the country's future, not on campaigning.
    Politics · UK
  • 2
    A Welsh police force has warned that a recent cyber attack may have 'compromised' information belonging to its staff.
    Dyfed-Powys Police say they don't believe any personal data relating to the public was accessed. An investigation is now underway to determine the full extent of the breach.
    Cyber Security · UK
  • 3
    No action has been taken against an Iranian man who attempted to enter the UK's nuclear submarine base earlier this year.
    The 34-year-old was arrested in March after trying to access the Faslane base in Argyll and Bute. It's home to the Royal Navy's nuclear submarine fleet.
    Defence · UK
  • 4
    Two men have been charged after police discovered an estimated ten million pounds worth of ketamine in a village.
    Police say the drugs were found in black bin bags. It's believed to be one of the largest hauls of the drug ever found in the UK.
    Crime · UK
  • 5
    An inquest has heard that a young boy left a playground through a gap in a fence before he was tragically killed.
    Noah Woods' mother has urged other parents to 'hold their children a little tighter and cherish every moment'. The inquest continues.
    Tragedy · UK
02 — Showbiz

What's coming up

  • 1
    The Turner Prize exhibition opens today, with work by four artists going on display in London.
    The prestigious art prize celebrates contemporary art, and the gallery is being hailed as a 'centre of the creative universe'. The winner will be announced later this year.
    Are you a fan of modern art, or do you prefer something more traditional?
    Art · This Weekend
  • 2
    The shortlist for this year's Booker Prize has been revealed, with two past winners among the nominees.
    Marlon James and Douglas Stuart are both in the running for the prestigious literary award. Judges described the selection process as a 'tussle' due to the high quality of entries.
    Have you read any of the shortlisted books, or do you have a favourite author you're hoping will win?
    Books · Coming Up
  • 3
    Catherine Zeta-Jones says she based her new role as a former cocaine dealer on funny Welsh people she knows.
    The actress plays Jackie Price in the upcoming film 'Kill Jackie', where her character is targeted by assassins. She revealed her inspiration during a recent interview.
    What's your favourite Catherine Zeta-Jones movie, or what kind of role would you love to see her play next?
    Film · Coming Up
  • 4
    Rapper Macklemore has announced a 'Free Palestine' tour, with proceeds going to organisations supporting Palestinians.
    The tour begins in Dublin next month, following a recent fallout with Ed Sheeran. Macklemore has been vocal about his support for the cause.
    Do you think artists should use their platform to raise awareness for political causes?
    Music · Coming Up

    An Indian film from Nagaland, 'Angh', is now on a path towards the Oscars after a major win at the Toronto Film Festival.
    The film brings a rarely seen world to global cinema, telling a powerful story from northeastern India. It's hoped to be a strong contender for international awards.
    What's the last international film you saw that really impressed you?
    Film · Coming Up

On the schedule

    Sources told the BBC that City has been found guilty of breaking the majority of the 115 financial rules they were charged with. The club has always denied any wrongdoing.

    Centurion Sam Hain shared a huge 165-run stand with skipper Ed Barnard. They're now just a few steps away from lifting the trophy.
    Are you a fan of county cricket, and who are you backing to win the Championship?

    It's old hands versus new blood in the Super League semi-final tonight, as Wigan and Wakefield vie to reach the Grand Final.
    Wigan have substantial play-off experience, while it's new territory for their opponents Wakefield. It promises to be an exciting clash.

    Wales boss Craig Bellamy believes there is 'more to come' from his side despite the loss. Liverpool forward Lewis Koumas earned praise for his efforts.

    Football · UK

04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    More of us are batch-cooking at the weekend to dodge the weeknight scramble.
    Freezers are filling with portioned dinners. The classic mistake: forgetting to label them, then playing 'mystery soup' on a Wednesday.
  • 2
    Cold-water taps for filling pets' bowls and watering cans are the surprise garden-centre hit.
    With more of us gardening, small convenience gadgets are booming — though the watering can remains undefeated.
  • 3
    The great British picnic is being upgraded, with sales of fold-out chairs and cool bags climbing.
    We're venturing out more, weather permitting. The traditional picnic still ends, as ever, with at least one item dropped in the grass.
  • 4
    Knitting and crochet are booming with a new generation, sold as the ultimate screen-free wind-down.
    Beginners start with scarves and quietly accumulate wool. The first project, makers admit, is usually 'a bit wonky but loved'.
  • 5
    A study says the average person walks past their phone over 50 times a day without picking it up.
    Researchers reckon just seeing it nearby is enough reassurance. The phone, meanwhile, remains entirely unbothered.
  • 6
    Allotment waiting lists have hit record highs across the UK.
    Councils in some areas now quote waits of several years. The most-grown first crop for beginners? Courgettes — closely followed by regret, as they produce far too many.
  • 7
    A survey finds most of us have at least one app we've never opened since downloading it.
    The average phone reportedly carries dozens of unused apps. Top of the 'meant to use it' pile: language-learning and meditation.
