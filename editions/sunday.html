04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    A new study suggests that taking a short 'mindfulness walk' can significantly boost creativity.
    Researchers found that even a ten-minute stroll, focusing on your senses, helped participants generate more innovative ideas afterwards. So, a quick wander could be the answer to writer's block!
  • 2
    The enduring popularity of 'retro video games' is seeing a surge, with more families gathering to play classic consoles.
    Experts believe the simple, often collaborative nature of older games offers a refreshing contrast to modern, complex titles. It’s all about shared fun and nostalgia!
  • 3
    A recent poll revealed that 'the smell of freshly baked bread' is the most comforting scent for Brits.
    It beat out other contenders like cut grass and rain on hot pavement. There's just something about that yeasty aroma that brings a sense of home and happiness.
  • 4
    The trend of 'upcycling old furniture' into new, stylish pieces is transforming homes across the country.
    People are getting creative with paint, fabric, and a bit of elbow grease, giving beloved but worn items a whole new lease of life. It’s good for the planet and your wallet!
  • 5
    A fascinating study found that houseplants can actually 'improve concentration' in home offices.
    Beyond just looking nice, the presence of greenery is thought to reduce stress and increase focus, making your workspace a little bit smarter. Time to get some leafy companions!
  • 6
    The simple pleasure of 'writing postcards' is experiencing a quiet revival as people seek more personal ways to connect.
    Moving away from instant messages, sending a handwritten card with a thoughtful message is becoming a cherished way to share holiday memories or just say hello. A lovely touch, isn't it?
  • 7
    More people are discovering the joy of 'outdoor cinema' nights, transforming gardens and parks into movie theatres under the stars.
    With projectors becoming more accessible, enjoying a favourite film al fresco with friends and family is a magical way to spend an evening. Just remember the blankets!
