04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    Gardeners are finding joy in cultivating 'microgreens' on their windowsills.
    These tiny, nutrient-packed plants like radish, rocket, and broccoli sprouts are easy to grow indoors, offering a fresh flavour boost to meals all year round. It's a lovely way to connect with nature, even in a small flat!
  • 2
    A delightful new trend sees people creating 'bespoke playlists' for their pets.
    Owners are curating calming tunes or upbeat melodies to suit their furry friends' moods, with some even reporting a noticeable difference in their pets' behaviour. Perhaps your dog prefers classical, or your cat a bit of jazz?
  • 3
    The humble 'charity shop' is becoming a treasure trove for unique home decor.
    Savvy shoppers are discovering vintage finds, quirky ornaments, and pre-loved furniture that add character and a personal touch to their living spaces. It's a sustainable way to refresh your home and support a good cause!
  • 4
    A fascinating study suggests that 'reading aloud' to yourself can boost memory.
    Researchers found that vocalising words helps to reinforce them in our minds, making information easier to recall. So, next time you're learning something new, don't be shy about having a little chat with yourself!
  • 5
    Many people are rediscovering the simple pleasure of 'writing postcards' whilst on holiday.
    In an age of instant messages, sending a handwritten postcard feels more personal and special, offering a lovely tangible memento for friends and family. It's a charming way to share your holiday adventures.
  • 6
    The art of 'pressed flower crafts' is seeing a beautiful revival.
    From creating intricate greeting cards to framing delicate botanical art, people are finding creative ways to preserve the beauty of flowers. It’s a wonderful, gentle hobby that brings the garden indoors.
  • 7
    More people are embracing the joy of 'slow walking' to enhance well-being.
    Instead of rushing, deliberately taking a slower pace allows for greater observation of surroundings and a more mindful experience. It’s a simple way to de-stress and appreciate the everyday.
