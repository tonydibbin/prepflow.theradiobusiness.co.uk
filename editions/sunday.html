04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    The fastest-growing hobby of 2026 is hand-embroidery — sales of needles and thread up 180% in a year.
    Crafters say it's the perfect 'phone detox' — slow, tactile, satisfying. TikTok is full of 20-somethings showing off custom denim jackets.
  • 2
    The average Brit checks their phone 159 times a day — but says it 'doesn't feel like that much'.
    Researchers using passive monitoring apps say we badly underestimate our own usage. Most people guess between 30 and 70 times.
  • 3
    Brits now spend an average of 14 hours a week on streaming — more than cooking, cleaning and exercising combined.
    The biggest jump is in the over-55s, who used to be the holdouts for live telly. 'Second-screening' means we pay less attention than ever.
  • 4
    Almost two thirds of us say we've cried at a podcast in the last year — grief, parenting and identity top the list of triggers.
    Researchers reckon it's the intimacy of audio that does it. The figure is even higher for under-30s, 71% of whom admit a recent podcast-induced tear.
  • 5
    More than half of UK workers would take a 10% pay cut to never go into the office again — but only 17% of bosses say they'd offer one.
    The gap is growing again after forced return-to-office rules. HR experts say the tension shows up in resignation rates, particularly for under-35s.
  • 6
    Cash is making a small comeback — ATM withdrawals are up 14% on this time last year.
    Bank analysts credit budget-conscious households using cash to stay disciplined. The 'envelope method' is back trending on TikTok.
  • 7
    Roses are out, peonies are in — UK florists report a 200% rise in peony orders for weddings this summer.
    Brides cite 'softer, less staged' aesthetics. Wholesale peony prices have crept up accordingly.
