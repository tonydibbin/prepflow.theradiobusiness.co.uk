    It’s a fantastic way to discover new authors and genres without spending a penny, fostering a lovely sense of community spirit around the love of reading.

    Researchers have found that looking at photos of cute animals can actually improve your focus and concentration.
    So, if you're feeling a bit distracted at work, a quick glance at a puppy or kitten picture might be just what you need to get back on track. Who knew cuteness was a productivity hack?
  • 6
    There's a growing interest in 'urban foraging', with city dwellers learning to identify edible plants and berries in parks and green spaces.
    It's a way to connect with nature right on your doorstep and discover unexpected natural treats, but always with caution and expert guidance!
