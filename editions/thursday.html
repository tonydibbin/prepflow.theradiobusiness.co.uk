04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    The most-Googled DIY question in Britain this year: 'how to remove silicone'.
    Tradespeople say it's the rise of botched bathroom resealing jobs during lockdown that's still haunting people. YouTube tutorials on the topic have racked up 60 million views.
  • 2
    Cash is making a small comeback — ATM withdrawals are up 14% on this time last year.
    Bank analysts credit budget-conscious households using cash to stay disciplined. The 'envelope method' is back trending on TikTok.
  • 3
    Britain's most-stolen supermarket item in 2026 isn't meat or cheese — it's olive oil, with theft up 220% year on year.
    Prices have nearly doubled in two years. Some shops have fitted security tags to single bottles, others have moved premium brands behind the counter — like cigarettes.
  • 4
    Brits now spend an average of 14 hours a week on streaming — more than cooking, cleaning and exercising combined.
    The biggest jump is in the over-55s, who used to be the holdouts for live telly. 'Second-screening' means we pay less attention than ever.
  • 5
    The 'silver gamer' is the fastest-growing demographic in UK gaming — over-65s spent £180 million on games last year.
    Cosy life-sim titles like Stardew Valley and Animal Crossing dominate. Console makers are reportedly designing larger UI for the market.
  • 6
    British baby names are getting shorter again — three- and four-letter names are dominating the registrar.
    Top picks this year: Noa, Ezra, Ada and Bo. Long, multi-syllable Victorian names like Theodora are sliding back down the chart.
  • 7
    Half of UK adults can't name a single member of the cabinet — but 80% can name three Love Island contestants.
    Political engagement researchers say it's not apathy so much as 'noise overload'. People remember faces from things they actively watch.
