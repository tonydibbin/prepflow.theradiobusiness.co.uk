04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    A new study suggests that taking a 'micro-break' to look at something green can boost your mood and productivity.
    Researchers found that even a quick glance at a plant or a garden view helps reduce stress and refocus the mind during a busy day. It seems a little bit of nature is a powerful pick-me-up!
  • 2
    The enduring appeal of 'jigsaw puzzles' is seeing a resurgence, with many finding them a perfect way to unwind and unplug.
    Sales of jigsaw puzzles have soared as people look for screen-free activities that offer a satisfying sense of accomplishment. It's a wonderful way to challenge the mind and relax at the same time.
  • 3
    Experts are encouraging us to embrace the simple joy of 'sketching' everyday objects.
    It doesn't require artistic talent, just a willingness to observe and capture the world around you. This mindful practice can improve focus and appreciation for the little details in life.
  • 4
    A growing number of people are discovering the delights of 'wild swimming' in natural bodies of water.
    From lakes to rivers and even the sea, the refreshing dip is praised for its invigorating benefits, both physically and mentally. Always remember to swim safely and check local conditions!
  • 5
    The trend of 'upcycling old furniture' is gaining popularity, as people transform pre-loved pieces into unique household items.
    Instead of buying new, many are enjoying the creative challenge of giving old chairs, tables, and cabinets a fresh lease of life. It’s good for the planet and your pocket!
  • 6
    Research indicates that 'listening to rain' can significantly improve sleep quality and reduce anxiety.
    The gentle, rhythmic sound of falling rain creates a 'pink noise' effect, which is thought to calm the brain and promote deeper, more restorative sleep. So, next rainy day, open a window!
  • 7
    The humble 'picnic' is being celebrated as a wonderful way to reconnect with nature and loved ones.
    Whether it's in a park, on the beach, or even in your own garden, packing a simple basket of goodies and eating outdoors offers a delightful break from routine. Don't forget the blanket!
