Quick hits

  • 1
    More households are growing herbs on the windowsill to cut grocery bills.

  • 2
    Swimming in the sea and lakes — 'wild swimming' — keeps growing in popularity year-round.
    Enthusiasts swear by the cold-water buzz. Newcomers are advised to start gently, and that the gasp on entry is completely normal.
  • 3
    Charity shops are reporting a surge in both donations and shoppers as 'pre-loved' goes mainstream.
    Younger shoppers in particular are hunting for one-off finds. Volunteers say the most donated item by a mile is the unworn 'someday' outfit.
  • 4
    Jigsaw puzzles are still selling in huge numbers, years after their lockdown boom.
    1,000-piece designs are the sweet spot. The most dreaded puzzle remains anything that's mostly sky.
  • 5
    Almost 70% of UK households now have a smart speaker — but the most common use is still 'asking what time it is'.
    Researchers say we under-use the devices we own. Setting timers, playing music and weather queries make up two thirds of all commands.
  • 6
    A study says the average person walks past their phone over 50 times a day without picking it up.
    Researchers reckon just seeing it nearby is enough reassurance. The phone, meanwhile, remains entirely unbothered.
  • 7
    Allotment waiting lists have hit record highs across the UK.
    Councils in some areas now quote waits of several years. The most-grown first crop for beginners? Courgettes — closely followed by regret, as they produce far too many.
