04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    British gardeners are finding a new joy in 'micro-gardening', cultivating tiny plants in unusual containers.
    From old teacups to miniature terrariums, enthusiasts are creating charming, diminutive green spaces perfect for windowsills and small flats. It's a delightful way to bring a bit of nature indoors.
  • 2
    The trend of 'urban foraging' is growing, with more Brits learning to identify edible plants in local parks and green spaces.
    Courses are popping up across the country, teaching people how to safely find wild garlic, elderflowers, and berries, reconnecting them with nature and providing free, fresh ingredients.
  • 3
    More people are embracing 'mindful walking' as a way to de-stress and appreciate their surroundings.
    It's not just about getting from A to B; it's about paying close attention to sights, sounds, and smells, turning a simple stroll into a refreshing mental break.
  • 4
    The simple pleasure of 'community book swaps' is flourishing, with little outdoor libraries appearing in neighbourhoods.
    These charming, often decorated, cabinets allow people to take a book and leave a book, fostering a sense of shared enjoyment and discovery within local communities.
  • 5
    A new appreciation for 'vintage board games' is bringing families and friends together for retro fun.
    Dusting off classics like 'Cluedo' and 'Monopoly', people are enjoying screen-free evenings filled with laughter and a touch of nostalgic competition.
  • 6
    British bakers are rediscovering the joy of 'sourdough starter sharing' amongst friends and neighbours.
    Instead of buying new, keen bakers are sharing a portion of their active starter, creating a lovely connection and ensuring delicious, homemade bread for everyone.
  • 7
    The delightful art of 'nature journaling' is helping people connect more deeply with the outdoors.
    Combining sketching, writing, and observation, it's a calm and creative way to document findings from a walk in the park or a trip to the countryside, encouraging mindfulness.
