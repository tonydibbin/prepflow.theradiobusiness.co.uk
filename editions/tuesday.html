04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    Scientists suggest that admiring a beautiful sunset can actually make you feel wealthier.
    Research indicates that moments of awe, like watching a colourful sky, can shift our focus from material wealth to time, making us feel more abundant in life's precious moments.
  • 2
    There's a growing trend for 'micro-adventures' – short, local escapades that don't break the bank.
    From wild swimming in a nearby lake to an overnight camp in the garden, these quick bursts of exploration offer all the benefits of adventure without the big travel plans.
  • 3
    A study reveals that people who regularly visit local markets tend to report higher levels of community connection.
    The bustling atmosphere and face-to-face interactions at markets foster a sense of belonging and support local traders, creating a lovely win-win.
  • 4
    The simple act of 'waving hello' to strangers is making a quiet comeback in some British villages.
    Often seen as a quaint, old-fashioned gesture, it's now being embraced as a small way to foster friendliness and neighbourly spirit, especially on morning walks.
  • 5
    New research suggests that the sound of a crackling fireplace can significantly reduce stress levels.
    The rhythmic, gentle sounds are thought to tap into ancient human instincts for comfort and safety, promoting a sense of calm and relaxation, even if it's just a recording.
  • 6
    The humble 'charity shop treasure hunt' is becoming a popular weekend activity for many.
    It's not just about finding a bargain; the thrill of discovering something unique and giving pre-loved items a new home is proving to be a delightful pastime for all ages.
  • 7
    Experts are encouraging us to rediscover the joy of 'writing postcards' when on holiday.
    In an age of instant digital messages, receiving a handwritten postcard through the letterbox is a charming surprise that brightens anyone's day and makes memories last longer.
