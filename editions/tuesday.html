Quick hits

  • 1
    British households are increasingly turning to 'upcycling' old furniture, giving beloved pieces a new lease of life.
    Instead of buying new, many are enjoying the creative process of sanding, painting, and restoring items, making them unique and environmentally friendly. It's a wonderful way to personalise your home.
  • 2
    The surprising popularity of 'plogging' is sweeping across UK parks and green spaces.
    This eco-friendly activity combines jogging with picking up litter, allowing people to get fit while doing good for the environment. It's a great way to make your run count for more than just steps!
  • 3
    A new trend sees people embracing the joy of 'micro-adventures' close to home.
    These are short, local escapades like an overnight camp in the garden, a wild swim, or exploring a nearby nature trail. It's about finding adventure without needing to travel far.
  • 4
    More of us are rediscovering the simple pleasure of handwritten letters and postcards.
    In an age of instant digital messages, receiving something tangible in the post feels extra special. It's a lovely way to connect with friends and family in a more personal way.
  • 5
    The delightful art of 'guerilla gardening' is bringing bursts of colour to neglected urban spots.
    People are planting flowers and plants in overlooked public areas, transforming grey corners into vibrant havens. It's a cheerful way to brighten up the neighbourhood for everyone.
  • 6
    Many Brits are finding new hobbies in traditional crafts, such as knitting, pottery, and woodworking.
    There's a growing appreciation for creating something with your own hands, offering a mindful escape from screens. These crafts often result in beautiful, handmade items too.
  • 7
    The humble picnic is enjoying a major revival across the UK, from city parks to country fields.
    People are embracing the simple joy of al fresco dining, complete with blankets, baskets, and tasty treats. It's a fantastic way to enjoy the great outdoors with friends and family.
