  • 5
    More people are embracing the charm of 'upcycled furniture' for their homes.
    Giving old pieces a new lease of life with a lick of paint or creative touches is not only sustainable but also adds unique character to any room.
  • 6
    Researchers are noting a trend towards 'conscious consumption' when it comes to everyday purchases.
    Shoppers are increasingly looking at the origin and environmental impact of products, favouring brands that align with their values and offer longevity.

    The delight of 'picnic planning' is bringing families and friends together for outdoor fun.
