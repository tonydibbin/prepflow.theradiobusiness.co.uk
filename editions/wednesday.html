04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    Britons are keeping their phones for longer than ever before upgrading.
    The average handset is now held onto for several years. The main reason cited isn't loyalty — it's the dread of moving everything across.
  • 2
    More people are writing 'done lists' instead of to-do lists to feel better about their day.
    Noting what you actually achieved, however small, apparently beats staring at everything you didn't. 'Got dressed' counts.
  • 3
    Cycling to the shops is up, with more people using bikes for short local trips rather than big rides.
    Councils credit better cycle lanes. The number one cargo carried? A surprisingly heavy single bag for life.
  • 4
    A study says the smell of fresh bread is one of the few things almost everyone finds comforting.
    Supermarkets have long known it — the in-store bakery is no accident. Few of us escape without an unplanned loaf.
  • 5
    Holiday firms report a 40% spike in 'shoulder season' bookings — September and October sun trips are way up on last year.
    Travellers blame school-holiday flight prices, unreliable summer weather, and 'August feels too crowded these days'. Spain, Greece and Portugal top the wish list.
  • 6
    Three quarters of dog owners now talk to their dog more than they talk to their partner.
    Top topics: 'what we're doing later', 'what we just saw out the window' and a surprising amount of Bake Off commentary. 12% admit they've apologised to the dog after a row with the human.
  • 7
    The most-Googled DIY question in Britain this year: 'how to remove silicone'.
    Tradespeople say it's the rise of botched bathroom resealing jobs during lockdown that's still haunting people. YouTube tutorials on the topic have racked up 60 million views.
