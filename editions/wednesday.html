04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    British dog owners are increasingly treating their furry friends to 'paw-dicures' and luxury grooming experiences.
    Forget a simple bath! Salons are offering everything from blueberry facials for pooches to glitter paw polish, ensuring our four-legged companions look their absolute best.
  • 2
    The trend of 'urban foraging' is growing, with city dwellers discovering edible plants and herbs in unexpected places.
    From wild garlic in local parks to forgotten fruit trees on public land, many are learning to identify and safely harvest nature's bounty right on their doorsteps.
  • 3
    More people are embracing 'slow travel', opting for longer, more immersive holidays closer to home.
    Instead of hopping between destinations, travellers are choosing to spend weeks exploring a single region, truly getting to know its culture and community at a relaxed pace.
  • 4
    The delightful sound of 'chime choirs' is becoming a popular new community activity across the UK.
    These groups use tuned hand chimes or bells to create beautiful, harmonious music, offering a calming and collaborative way for people of all ages to make music together.
  • 5
    A new appreciation for 'vintage technology' is emerging, with many collecting and restoring old radios, record players, and typewriters.
    It's a step back from digital overload, as enthusiasts enjoy the tactile experience and unique charm of gadgets from yesteryear, often finding them more satisfying to use.
  • 6
    British households are increasingly experimenting with 'vertical gardening' to maximise small spaces.
    From wall-mounted herb gardens to stacked planters for vegetables, this innovative approach allows even those with tiny patios or balconies to grow an impressive array of plants.
  • 7
    The simple joy of 'board game cafes' is seeing a resurgence across the country, offering a fun alternative to traditional pubs.
    These cosy spots provide a vast library of games, from classics to new releases, alongside refreshments, creating a relaxed social hub for friends and families.
