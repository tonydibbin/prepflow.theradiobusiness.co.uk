04 — Newsbrief

Quick hits

  • 1
    British gardeners are finding joy in 'cottagecore' aesthetics, turning their plots into whimsical, abundant havens.
    It's all about natural, slightly wild planting, with an emphasis on colourful flowers, edible plants, and a charming, rustic feel. Think less manicured lawns and more relaxed, overflowing borders.
  • 2
    The simple pleasure of stargazing is making a comeback across the UK.
    With more dark sky reserves and accessible apps, families and individuals are rediscovering the wonder of the night sky, away from city lights. It’s a lovely, peaceful way to reconnect with nature.
  • 3
    A new appreciation for 'heritage crafts' is emerging, with many Brits learning traditional skills.
    From basket weaving to blacksmithing, people are keen to master crafts passed down through generations. It offers a tangible connection to history and a sense of accomplishment.
  • 4
    The trend of 'library tourism' is quietly growing, as people seek out beautiful and historic libraries to visit.
    These architectural gems offer more than just books; they're often stunning spaces for reflection and cultural exploration. It’s a wonderful way to experience a city’s intellectual heart.
  • 5
    British homes are increasingly embracing 'biophilic design', bringing more of the outdoors inside.
    This involves incorporating natural light, plants, and natural materials like wood and stone into interiors. The aim is to create a calming, health-boosting environment that feels connected to nature.
  • 6
    The delightful art of 'gifting experiences' rather than physical presents is gaining popularity.
    Instead of another gadget, people are opting for concert tickets, cookery classes, or weekend getaways. It creates lasting memories and reduces clutter, which is a win-win!
  • 7
    More people are discovering the meditative benefits of 'forest bathing', or Shinrin-yoku, in local woodlands.
    It's not about hiking, but simply being present and immersing your senses in the atmosphere of the forest. Many report feeling more relaxed and refreshed afterwards.
